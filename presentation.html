Prospecting eats your whole week.

Stages 1 & 2

Build your target list

01 — Organizations

Search by domain, company name, or filters like industry, size and location — or bulk-upload an Excel list. Each company is auto-enriched and saved to your CRM.

02 — People

For any company, surface the relevant decision-makers — enriched with role, seniority and LinkedIn — and turn them into leads in one click.

You vet every company and contact — open their website and LinkedIn — then keep only what's worth it. Nothing is saved blindly.

The core

AI writes every email

  • A personalized draft per lead, built from their company and role.
  • A real sequence: first mail, follow-ups, final touch.
  • Nothing sends until you approve it — every single mail, by hand.
  • Once you approve, it sends the email and advances the CRM stage.
Stage 4

Every reply, tracked

  • Incoming replies are watched automatically — no inbox babysitting.
  • Each reply is matched back to the right lead and person.
  • The CRM label flips to “answered” with zero manual logging.
  • Every response shows up in one place in the app.

The loop closes itself.
